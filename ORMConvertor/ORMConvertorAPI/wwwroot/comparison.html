This page is a mockup

The artifacts are real; the connections between them are not the tool’s. Everything in the windows below is one recorded run of the seven examples of the explanatory page — captured on 2026‑09‑23 from commit 4042360, against an instance with a database catalog configured — frozen into the page, so nothing here calls the server and nothing here is converted while you read it.

The tool does not report which word of the output came from which word of the input. Its records say what could not be carried, what a convention filled in and what the database supplied; they never say what travelled and where it landed. Every connection on this page was therefore written by hand, to show what such a screen could look like if the pipeline carried that information. Not every word is connected: only what was worth pointing at.

Why this exists, and what it is material for, is decision 100 and the open item Translation direction as one thing and input beside output. Nothing on this page is part of what the tool claims.
